## The brief

Build an ESG (environmental, social and governance) management platform, end to end, in 8 hours.

## What we shipped

- Carbon tracking
- Challenge gamification with XP and badges
- Policy acknowledgement
- An ESG simulator
- Cross-department reporting

Each role (employee, manager, ESG manager) gets its own dashboard.

## My part

- Led the database schema design and coordinated the team.
- Directed the Git workflow and the repository structure.
- Guided the React frontend.
