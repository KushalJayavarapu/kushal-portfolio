## The problem

Apps and websites update, and agents that used them break. AdaptBank measures how well an LLM agent holds up when its environment changes, so a developer choosing an agent can compare numbers instead of guessing.

## The framework

AdaptBank is a unified evaluation framework for the environmental robustness of AI agents. Its taxonomy covers four types of drift:

- **Semantic drift**
- **Interface drift**
- **Workflow drift**
- **Policy drift**

Agents are scored on three metrics: Robustness Score, Recovery Performance Score and Context Adaptation Time.

## What I did

I worked on AdaptBank during a six-week summer research internship at PES University's Center for Cloud Computing and Big Data (CCBD).

- Evaluated agent robustness under Semantic drift by running the ToolMisuseBench suite.
- Computed all three metrics and benchmarked 6 agents, including 2 frontier models.
- Contributed cross-agent scoring to a standardized Environmental Robustness Score, meant to power an agent leaderboard.
- Extended coverage to further drift suites (OpenApps, WebArena and tau-bench) spanning Interface, Workflow and Policy drift.

The biggest obstacle was API rate limits: getting through one benchmarking run took 10 to 15 API keys.

## Where it stands

The work is continuing informally with the team toward a research paper, which is awaiting publication approval.
