## The problem

The project started from the "Golden Hour" after road accidents: the response gap between a crash and help arriving. We validated that gap with real people before proposing anything.

## What we did

- Conducted 60+ customer interviews across multiple states, with commuters, parents and long-distance drivers.
- Used them to identify and validate the Golden Hour response gap.
- Proposed a sensor-based IoT accident detection and emergency alert system.

This was a third-semester project.

## Result

HustleHub was one of the Top 13 finalists out of 77 teams at CIE Spark 2025.
